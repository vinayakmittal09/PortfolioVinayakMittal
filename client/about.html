About

I'm a creative who works
between technology
and visual storytelling.

Twelve years ago I picked up a camera to remember a trip. I never really put it down.

My work moves between still photography, motion and design — not because I couldn't pick a lane, but because the best stories rarely fit in just one medium. Some briefs need a single quiet frame. Others need thirty seconds of sound and motion. I try to bring the same eye to both.

I've spent the last decade shooting portraits and documentary work, directing brand films, cutting reels for clients who need to move fast, and designing the identities that hold it all together. I care more about a frame that feels true than one that's technically perfect.

Based in Lisbon, working wherever the story is.
